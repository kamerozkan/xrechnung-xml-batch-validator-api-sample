## Privacy and security

This repository contains no customer invoice, raw XML, PDF, base64 payload, extracted invoice XML, report body, access token, cookie, signed URL, webhook URL, email address, IBAN, tax identifier, signature, or customer account identifier.

The public fixtures can contain synthetic invoice values under their upstream terms. The sample output projections exclude invoice parties, addresses, bank details, tax identifiers, amounts, and line items.

Validation findings can quote an offending invoice value in customer runs. Optional XML, HTML, PDF/A, and extracted-invoice records can contain full invoice data. Users remain responsible for lawful processing, access control, retention, deletion, and applicable privacy, tax, accounting, database, and contractual requirements.

## Interpretation limits

- `ACCEPTED` means the submitted bytes passed every required technical validation layer available under the pinned rule stack.
- `REJECTED` means the document was processed and at least one required technical rule failed.
- `NOT_EVALUATED` means a processing failure prevented a technical decision.
- A technical result is not proof of legal or tax validity.
- A technical result is not proof of authenticity, authorization, delivery, payment, or accounting treatment.
- A hybrid PDF result does not establish that its visible PDF content and embedded XML have identical business meaning unless that relationship is separately verified.
- A result does not guarantee that a recipient, access point, tax authority, ERP, or accounting workflow will accept the invoice.

The repository provides no completeness, accuracy, uptime, freshness, legal-compliance, or service-level guarantee. Check the Actor's current Store page, pricing, input schema, supported profiles, rule versions, and limits before production use.
